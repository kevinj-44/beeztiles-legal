Privacy Policy — Beez Tiles

Effective date: 2026-07-28 · Last updated: 2026-07-28

This Privacy Policy explains how The BeeZ Studio (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with the mobile game Beez Tiles (the “App”, package com.beeztiles.game). By using the App you agree to this policy.

In short: Beez Tiles is a fully offline block-puzzle game. You do not need an account or login, and there are no in-app purchases. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. The only way information leaves your device is through advertising (Google AdMob), and only while you play the ad-supported Classic mode.

1. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide your name, email, or address to play. The App does not ask you to type a nickname, upload photos, or share contacts. The App handles the following:

  • Device & usage data (automatic, via ads). Through the third-party advertising service below, we and our provider may process your device advertising identifier (GAID), device model, operating system version, app version, language/region, IP address (used for approximate country/city-level location and fraud prevention), and ad-interaction events (ad impressions and clicks).
  • Game progress & settings (on your device). Your level progress, best scores, Hive level/XP, daily and weekly records, and preferences are stored locally on your device. If Android Auto Backup is enabled on your device, this local data may be backed up to your Google account and restored when you reinstall the App.
  • Anonymous local metrics (on your device). The App keeps anonymous event counts on the device to help us fix bugs and balance difficulty. These stay on the device and are not transmitted to us.

We do not knowingly collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise GPS location.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the App, and to save and restore your progress on your device.
  • To display advertising and keep the App free to play.
  • To measure ad performance and to detect and prevent ad fraud or abuse.
  • To improve gameplay and fix issues using on-device anonymous metrics.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland, our legal bases are: performance of a contract (providing the game), your consent (personalized advertising, where applicable), and our legitimate interests (security and fraud prevention).

3. Third-party services

The App uses the following Google services, which may collect and process data as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — How Google uses data · Google Privacy Policy · AdMob advertising partners
  • Google Play services (app distribution and updates) — Google Privacy Policy

The App does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs (such as Google Analytics for Firebase), and does not use Google Play Billing, because it has no in-app purchases.

4. Advertising

The App is free and ad-supported. Advertising is shown by Google AdMob and appears only in the Classic (3D) mode — a bottom banner during play and a full-screen ad when a Classic run ends. The Challenge mode is ad-free. Where required in your region (for example, the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), personalized ads are shown only where permitted; otherwise non-personalized ads are shown. You can reset your advertising identifier and opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings (see Section 9).

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not “sell” or “share” it for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under California law (CCPA/CPRA), except for the use of advertising identifiers by our advertising provider as described above. We may disclose information:

  • to the third-party service providers listed above, who process it to serve ads and distribute the App;
  • to comply with applicable law, legal process, or a lawful government request;
  • to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or others, and to prevent fraud or abuse;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, in which case any acquirer will be bound by this policy with respect to your information.

6. International data transfers

Our advertising provider (Google) operates globally, so information processed for ads may be processed and stored on servers located outside your country of residence, including in the United States, where data-protection laws may differ. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards (for example, the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses) implemented by that provider.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the age of digital consent in their region — generally under 13 (United States), under 16 (EEA), and under 14 (Republic of Korea) — and we do not serve them personalized ads. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

8. Data retention

Game progress and settings are retained on your device until you reset them in the App (Settings → Reset all data) or uninstall the App (subject to any Google account backup you have enabled). Data processed by Google AdMob is retained according to Google’s policies, only for as long as necessary for the purposes described.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier, and opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Local data. Use Settings → Reset all data in the App, or uninstall the App, to remove locally stored game data from the device.
  • Access / deletion. Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPA in Korea), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us using the details below and we will respond as required by applicable law.

10. Security

We rely on the security measures of the platforms and services we use (Google Play, AdMob) and take reasonable measures to protect information. Because your game data is stored on your own device and we operate no user database, we do not hold your personal data on our servers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. In the event of a data breach that affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable law.
